# sharpen

**When**: chalach says "sharpen", or a brief looks shallow before launch. Deeper, not bigger: the size and the budget
stay the same.

## Before rewriting, answer four questions (one line each)
1. What would a top expert in this field do here that the brief does not ask for?
2. What raw material would they start from instead of a digest? (The raw data file, the vendor's curve, the paper's
   figure, the measured sweep.)
3. Which methods and tools would they use? (A fit with error bars, a local solver checked against a closed form, a
   fake-data harness, before and after with the same method.)
4. What, other than the agent itself, would show the result is right? (A measurement, a test that fails on the old
   code, a second independent source.)

## Then
- Rewrite the brief to ask exactly that. Keep "Do only this" and the step limit (S: 2, M: 4).
- Leave chalach's choices open. Do not decide them; list them under "Questions for chalach".
- Show chalach the four answers and the new brief before launch.
